The North Ecliptic Pole region runs on 120 real NASA SPHEREx QR2 FITS exposures from IPAC IRSA cross-matched with Gaia DR3. For our 5 target stars (Barnard's Star, Epsilon Indi, WISE 0855-0714, RW Aurigae, and R Draconis), IRSA's TAP/SIA servers were returning 502 errors during download, so the app preserves scientific honesty by displaying their real Gaia/SIMBAD coordinates and a physically modeled Synthetic Fixture preview—and automatically upgrades any region to real calibrated photometry the moment IRSA cutouts are exported.
